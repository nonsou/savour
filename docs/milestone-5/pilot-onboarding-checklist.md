## Per-slot steps

Copy this list per slot. Record a date and the evidence location for each step; leave it blank until it actually happens.

### 1. Authorized invitation, then consent before setup

- [ ] SAVOUR owner explicitly authorizes contacting this candidate (date and authorization record).
- [ ] After authorized contact, explain the pilot and name the partner-side owner. Record their consent to the pilot terms in the private record before account setup or real-data work.
- [ ] No candidate message is sent before SAVOUR owner authorization; this checklist itself sends nothing.

### 2. Workspace setup (Get started)

- [ ] Workspace created for the partner (country and timezone confirmed with them).
- [ ] Roles agreed and invited from **Team**: who is Owner, who assesses, who reviews. Optional reviewer step noted.
- [ ] **Add a location**: the real locations agreed with the partner.
- [ ] **Install a starter pack** matching the slot's segment (or publish an agreed template). Criteria reviewed with the partner and edits recorded.
- [ ] **Add subjects**, one at a time or through the CSV preview/import. Check the preview before import.

### 3. Practice with SAMPLE (never counted)

- [ ] **Start a SAMPLE inspection** from the overview: assess, review and approve the practice session.
- [ ] Open the SAMPLE report and confirm the partner understands scores, findings and approval. SAMPLE sessions never count toward totals, dashboards, setup progress or any pilot metric.

### 4. First real inspection (separately approved)

- [ ] Partner owner explicitly approves starting the first real inspection (date, record). This is separate from the consent in step 1.
- [ ] **Create and open a real session**, labelled in the private record as the first real inspection.
- [ ] **Approve your first real report**. Keep the approval and report revision evidence; do not alter approved records.

### 5. Repeat cycle and evidence

- [ ] Agreed inspection cycle recorded (for example, weekly or monthly) and the repeat inspection completed in that cycle.
- [ ] Baseline report effort measured before using SAVOUR (how long the partner's current report takes), with the method written down.
- [ ] Report effort measured on the real SAVOUR reports with the same method.
- [ ] Activation evidence: dates of first real approval and repeat inspection, with the denominator (slots that started), not a percentage inferred from invitations.
- [ ] Payment evidence, only if a priced offer was separately approved by the owner: offer, acceptance and verified payment record. No payment is assumed from intent.

## Not covered here

- Real data migration, restore drills and paid/live billing activation: separate gates in `pilot-preparation.md`.
- Any response-time or service-level promise. Development warnings reach the owner through the admin inbox and verified email delivery. Recorded weekday availability does not establish response deadlines, after-hours coverage or a changed sending schedule.
